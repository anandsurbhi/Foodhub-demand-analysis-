1. Problem Context

FoodHub is a food delivery aggregator that connects customers to multiple restaurants across New York City via a smartphone app. The company stores data on each completed order. The goal of this analysis is to uncover actionable insights that help:

  • Understand demand patterns across cuisines, days, and restaurants
  • Assess customer satisfaction via ratings
  • Identify bottlenecks in preparation and delivery time
  • Provide data-backed business recommendations

# ── Feature Engineering ───────────────────────────────────────────────────
# Numeric rating
df['rating_num'] = pd.to_numeric(df['rating'], errors='coerce')

# Total delivery time (prep + delivery)
df['total_time'] = df['food_preparation_time'] + df['delivery_time']

# Revenue bucket
df['cost_bucket'] = pd.cut(df['cost_of_the_order'],
                           bins=[0,10,20,30,40],
                           labels=['<$10','$10-20','$20-30','$30+'])

print("New columns added: rating_num, total_time, cost_bucket")

5. Actionable Insights & Recommendations

Key Insights

  • Weekend concentration risk: 71.2% of orders fall on weekends. Revenue is vulnerable to weekend disruptions.
  • High rating dropout: 38.8% of customers don't rate orders, suppressing quality signals.
  • Top-5 restaurant dependency: 5 restaurants drive 33.2% of revenue — a churn risk.
  • Delivery speed drives satisfaction: Orders rated 3 average 52.5 min total; rated 5 average 51.2 min.
  • Premium segment untapped: 29.2% of orders exceed $20 but receive no differentiated service.
  • Cuisine diversity gap: Thai, French, Vietnamese represent under 2.5% of orders combined, despite demand.

Recommendations

  • Launch weekday-specific promotions (corporate meal plans, office delivery bundles) to grow weekday share to 40%+.
  • Incentivize ratings with loyalty points to reduce the 38.8% "Not given" rate.
  • Actively onboard restaurants in underrepresented cuisine categories.
  • Introduce a "FoodHub Express" premium tier ($2-3/order) targeting the 29% high-value segment.
  • Monitor and intervene on orders approaching 50-minute mark to protect NPS.
  • Publish a monthly performance dashboard for restaurant partners.
